# Getting started with Implementation Pro

## Open your workspace

After you sign in, you land in your organization’s workspace. If you are new, you may complete onboarding first: create your organization, then add an engagement or skip and add one later.

## What an engagement is

An engagement is one customer implementation. It has tabs for scope, people, checklists, operations data, and AI helpers. You switch engagements from the Engagements list.

## Where to get help

Use **Help & Support** in the left menu for the assistant. Owners and admins also have **Admin Data Studio** for safe data previews and exports.
